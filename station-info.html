STATION INFORMATION

CALL SIGN:WTLEB
FREQUENCY:1720 AM
SERVICE:EMERGENCY INFORMATION / CONTINUITY BROADCAST
OPERATING STATUS:ACTIVE

WTLEB maintains broadcast capability for the distribution of emergency information during periods in which conventional communication infrastructure is unavailable, unreliable, or no longer considered authoritative.

Available station records indicate that WTLEB has operated under multiple continuity procedures, relay configurations, and emergency authorities. Documentation concerning the original establishment of the station is incomplete. Surviving records do not provide a consistent date for the beginning of transmission.

Several archived materials reference WTLEB operations prior to the earliest confirmed station records. The origin of these materials has not been verified.

Current transmissions are distributed through available terrestrial and external relay infrastructure. Broadcast routing may change without notice in response to signal conditions, infrastructure loss, or other operational requirements.

The station continues to transmit under standing emergency authority.
Records identifying the issuing authority are unavailable.

Personnel information, transmitter locations, and operational facilities are not published through this system.

Listeners receiving WTLEB transmissions are advised to retain access to available relay channels and to monitor this frequency for further information.
